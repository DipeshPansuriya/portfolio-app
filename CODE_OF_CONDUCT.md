# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good place to work:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, review comments) and when
an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [DipeshPansuriya](https://github.com/DipeshPansuriya), through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/DipeshPansuriya/portfolio-app/security/advisories/new) once it is
available. If it is not, open an issue that only asks for a private channel, with no details. Reports are reviewed
and handled confidentially.

Possible responses, scaled to severity:

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor, first occurrence | private warning with a request to stop |
| Repeated or moderate | temporary removal from interaction |
| Severe or sustained | permanent ban from project spaces |
